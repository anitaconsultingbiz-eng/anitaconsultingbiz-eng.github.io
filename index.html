Student support · Process design

Academic Support Program for Graduate Students on Probation

Problem

Graduate students who were conditionally admitted or placed on academic probation received warning letters, but there was no structured support system to help them improve academically.

Students were expected to figure things out on their own, which created inconsistent support across programs.

What I Did

I identified the gap and proposed a structured support process for at-risk graduate students.

I worked with Program Directors and the Center for Learning and Academic Success (CLAS) to create a process that included:

  • Academic coaching referrals
  • Updated communication workflows
  • Updates to admission and probation letters
  • Shared tracking between advisors and coaches

I coordinated discussions across departments and helped implement the process.

Result

The process was officially adopted. Students on probation or admitted conditionally were connected to academic coaching and support services early in the process.

This created a more consistent and proactive support system for graduate students.

  • Slate
  • Navigate

Data organization · Forecasting

Online Course Tracking & Enrollment Forecasting System

Problem

The department did not have a centralized way to track online course demand, student progression, or future scheduling needs.

Information was spread across multiple systems, making it difficult to forecast course demand and plan future semesters.

What I Did

I built an Excel-based tracking system that combined course, student, and enrollment data into one place.

The system tracked:

  • Course offerings by semester
  • Student progression
  • Leaves of absence
  • Graduation timelines
  • Projected course demand

I used reports from multiple university systems and cleaned the data using Power Query in Excel.

Result

The department gained a centralized system for tracking online course demand and planning future course offerings.

The process improved visibility into enrollment needs and reduced manual tracking work.

  • Slate
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Power Query

Research · Cross-department coordination

STEM Designation Support for MBA Business Intelligence Concentrations

Problem

International students in non-STEM business programs had limited access to extended work authorization opportunities after graduation.

The MBA Online Program wanted to determine whether its Business Intelligence concentrations qualified for STEM designation.

What I Did

I worked with the Provost’s Office and academic leadership to support the STEM designation process.

To determine whether the concentrations qualified, I reviewed the six-digit Classification of Instructional Programs (CIP) codes on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List.

I compared the applicable CIP code descriptions with the program curriculum, including:

  • Course descriptions
  • Syllabi
  • Learning outcomes
  • Curriculum structures
  • Technical and analytical content

I identified the CIP codes that applied and worked with the Office of Institutional Research & Assessment to review the information and approve the selection.

I also coordinated with departments involved in international student support.

Result

The Business Intelligence concentrations received STEM designation.

This allowed eligible international students to apply for the 24-month STEM OPT extension and improved the competitiveness of the program.

Systems & Resources

  • DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List
  • Colleague UI
  • Microsoft Excel

Program design · Community education

Community Appraisal Literacy & ROV Education Program

Problem

Community members often did not understand how appraisals worked, how property values were determined, or when a Reconsideration of Value (ROV) request made sense.

There was no curriculum, learning structure, or delivery plan in place.

What I Did

I designed and built a multi-module workshop that explained:

  • The appraisal process
  • How to review appraisal reports
  • How to verify property information
  • When an ROV request was appropriate

I created learning modules, real-world examples, participant tools, a Verification Log, and a structured learning path.

I also set up the program in Google Classroom so participants could access materials and activities in one place.

Result

The project turned a broad idea into a structured community education program.

Participants gained a better understanding of appraisal reports and felt more confident identifying legitimate concerns before requesting an ROV.

Systems Used

  • Google Classroom
  • Google Workspace
