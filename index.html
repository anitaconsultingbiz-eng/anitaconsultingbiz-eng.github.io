About Anita

I see the connections between people, processes, information, and tools.

My role is to make those connections easier to manage.

I bring more than 25 years of experience in higher education and program operations, where much of my work happened behind the scenes—coordinating programs, supporting faculty and students, managing schedules and information, anticipating problems, navigating changing priorities, and making sure the many moving pieces came together.

That experience shaped how I approach my work today. I have learned that the problem someone initially brings to me is not always the problem that ultimately needs solving. I listen, ask questions, look at how the work is actually being done, and identify where information, processes, responsibilities, or tools are getting in the way.

My consulting work has expanded that approach into documentation, training, websites, grant tracking, dashboards, workflow tools, and operational systems. I am comfortable stepping into unfamiliar work, learning what I need to learn, and using both established tools and newer technologies—including AI—to develop practical solutions.

I am especially drawn to working with solopreneurs, small and growing businesses, nonprofits, and professionals who are carrying a lot themselves. You may know your work extremely well but simply need someone who can help organize what is happening around it, build the structure that is missing, or turn an idea into something you can actually use.

I don't believe every problem needs a complicated system. Sometimes the right solution is a clearer process, a better way to organize information, a useful tool, or simply someone who can see how the pieces fit together.
